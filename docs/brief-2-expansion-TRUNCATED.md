# PROJECT: LANDVERIFY
## A New Standard for Land Due Diligence and Transactions in India

### Master Product Expansion Prompt

Act as an elite product designer, creative director, UX researcher, product strategist, software architect and senior full-stack engineer.

You have already received my original product brief and may have implemented parts of the application. Your task is to inspect the existing project, understand what is already present, and extend it into a scalable Indian land due diligence and transaction platform.

**Do not rebuild the application from scratch. Do not discard working features, existing data, established components or the original visual direction.**

Preserve the original design philosophy inspired by Apple's design psychology, combining:

- **Quiet Precision** for the public marketing website.
- **The Digital Deal Room** for the authenticated application.

The new requirement is to make the complete land transaction lifecycle part of the product architecture from day one, even though the initial release focuses primarily on due diligence.

The platform should eventually support property intelligence, document verification, collaborative reviews, transaction preparation, stamp duty assessment, stamp paper and e-stamping, registration automation, and post-registration mutation tracking.

The product must feel like a complete platform whose capabilities are released progressively, not a due diligence application that will later need to be redesigned into a transaction platform.

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The guiding idea is:

**Make complicated land decisions feel clear.**

Every screen should answer three questions:

1. What am I looking at?
2. What does it mean for this property or transaction?
3. What should I do next?

### Visual direction

Retain the original design system:

- Warm white background: approximately `#FAFAF8`.
- Near-black primary text: approximately `#171717`.
- Neutral secondary text: approximately `#777777`.
- Deep blue primary action: approximately `#174EA6`.
- Restrained neutral surfaces.
- Muted green for success, amber for warnings and accessible red for critical risks.
- Refined sans-serif typography such as Inter.
- Generous whitespace and strong alignment.
- Thin borders and restrained corner radii.
- Minimal shadows.
- Clear typography hierarchy.
- Monospaced typography for selected property IDs, transaction references and audit identifiers.

Use color to communicate meaning, never as a substitute for text labels.

Avoid generic real estate dashboards, excessive KPI cards, decorative gradients, cryptocurrency aesthetics, unnecessary floating elements and repetitive card layouts.
